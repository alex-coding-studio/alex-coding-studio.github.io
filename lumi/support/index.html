中文

鲁米为家长整理宝宝的体检测量与生长变化。

为什么某个年龄没有参考曲线？

WHO 与 CDC 的不同标准、指标有各自适用的年龄和参考人群。缺少适用参考数据时，鲁米会显示不可用状态。

切换 WHO 与 CDC 会改变原始测量吗？

不会。切换的是图表使用的参考资料，孩子的原始测量记录保持不变。

百分位能用于判断孩子是否健康吗？

鲁米用于记录与参考，不提供诊断或治疗建议。生长曲线和百分位不能单独用于判断健康状况；如有疑问，请咨询儿科医生。

如何反馈问题？

请附上 App 版本、iPhone 型号、iOS 版本、问题发生前的操作步骤和错误提示。请勿发送孩子的姓名、生日或完整健康记录；截图中如有个人信息，请先遮挡。

English

Lumi helps parents review their child’s checkup measurements and growth over time.

Why is a growth reference unavailable?

WHO and CDC standards cover specific ages, measurements and reference populations. Lumi shows an unavailable state when there is no applicable reference.

Does switching WHO and CDC change the original measurements?

No. It changes the reference used by the chart. Your child’s original measurements stay unchanged.

Can percentiles determine whether a child is healthy?

Lumi is for recordkeeping and reference. It does not provide diagnosis or treatment advice. Growth charts and percentiles alone cannot determine a child’s health. Discuss medical concerns with your child’s pediatrician.

How do I report an issue?

Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, steps leading to the issue and any error message. Avoid sending a child’s name, date of birth or complete health records. Redact personal information from screenshots.

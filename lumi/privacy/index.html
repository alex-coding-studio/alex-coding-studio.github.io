中文

鲁米是一款面向家长的儿童健康档案 App，由 Alex Coding Studio 提供。

你输入的档案和记录

鲁米在设备上保存你录入的孩子档案和体检测量等个人记录，用于展示和回顾孩子的生长变化。生长参考资料为 App 随附的只读数据，与个人记录分开存储。

私人 iCloud 同步

iCloud 可用时，个人记录通过 Apple CloudKit 与你的私人 iCloud 数据库同步，以便在使用同一 Apple 账户的设备间保持更新。这些记录可能因此离开本机，但不会发送到 Alex Coding Studio 的服务器，开发者也不能通过 App 的私人 CloudKit 数据库读取这些记录。

开发者不收集健康记录，不追踪用户

鲁米不向开发者发送你的孩子档案或体检测量，不包含广告、分析或跨 App 追踪 SDK，也不出售这些数据。

你主动联系支持时

如果你通过邮件联系我们，你主动提供的邮箱地址、文字和附件会用于处理支持请求。请避免发送孩子的姓名、生日、完整健康记录或其他不必要的敏感信息。

外部资料与 Apple 服务

App 可以打开 WHO、CDC 等官方资料网页。访问这些网站或使用 iCloud 时，相应服务适用其自身的隐私政策与条款。本政策说明鲁米 App 的数据处理方式，不替代 Apple 或外部网站的政策。

家长与儿童资料

鲁米由家长使用和管理孩子的资料，不是面向儿童的社交服务。请仅录入你有权管理的孩子资料。

管理数据

你可以通过设备与 Apple 提供的 iCloud 存储管理功能管理相应数据。从设备卸载 App 不一定会删除私人 iCloud 中的记录。开发者无法代为查看或删除你的私人 iCloud 健康记录；如有管理方面的问题，请联系我们。

政策更新与联系

数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新本页及最后更新日期。如有问题，请联系 alexcodingstudio@gmail.com。

English

Lumi is a child health record app for parents, provided by Alex Coding Studio.

Profiles and records you enter

Lumi stores child profiles, checkup measurements and related personal records on your device to help you review your child’s growth. Bundled growth references are read-only resources stored separately from personal records.

Private iCloud sync

When iCloud is available, personal records sync through Apple CloudKit to your private iCloud database so they can stay up to date across devices using the same Apple Account. These records may therefore leave your device, but are not sent to Alex Coding Studio servers. The developer cannot read these records through the app’s private CloudKit database.

No developer collection of health records or tracking

Lumi does not send your child profiles or checkup measurements to the developer. It contains no advertising, analytics or cross-app tracking SDKs, and does not sell this data.

Contacting support

If you email us, the email address, text and attachments you voluntarily provide are used to respond to your support request. Avoid sending a child’s name, date of birth, complete health records or other unnecessary sensitive information.

External resources and Apple services

The app can open official WHO and CDC resources. Those websites and Apple’s iCloud services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. This policy describes the Lumi app’s data handling and does not replace those policies.

Parents and child records

Parents use Lumi to manage child records. Lumi is not a social service for children. Only enter records you are authorized to manage.

Managing data

You can manage the relevant data using device and iCloud storage controls provided by Apple. Uninstalling the app does not necessarily delete records in private iCloud storage. The developer cannot view or delete your private iCloud health records on your behalf. Contact us if you need help with data management.

Policy updates and contact

We will update this page and its last-updated date when our data practices change. For questions, email alexcodingstudio@gmail.com.

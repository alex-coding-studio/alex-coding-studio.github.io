中文

GalleryX 是一款完全在本机运行的相册整理工具。原则很简单：你的照片是你的。

不收集数据

GalleryX 不收集、不存储、不上传你的任何个人信息或照片。

本地处理

浏览、整理、清理全部在你的设备上完成，照片永远不会离开本机，也不会发送到我们或任何第三方服务器。

相册权限

仅用于在本机呈现照片供你整理；写入权限仅用于把你选择的照片归类到相册。你可随时在系统设置中调整。

统计数据

清理成果（如删除数量）仅保存在你的设备本地。

无第三方追踪

GalleryX 不含任何分析、广告或追踪 SDK。

联系

有任何疑问，请发邮件至 alexcodingstudio@gmail.com

English

GalleryX is a photo-organizing tool that runs entirely on your device. The principle is simple: your photos are yours.

No data collection

GalleryX does not collect, store, or upload any of your personal information or photos.

On-device processing

Browsing, organizing, and cleanup all happen on your device. Your photos never leave it and are never sent to us or any third-party server.

Photo access

Used only to show your photos on-device so you can organize them; write access is used only to sort photos you choose into albums. You can adjust this anytime in system Settings.

Statistics

Cleanup results, such as deletion counts, are stored only locally on your device.

No third-party tracking

GalleryX contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

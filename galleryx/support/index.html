使用 GalleryX 时遇到问题？我们会帮助你继续整理。

中文

GalleryX 在你的设备上直接整理照片，不需要账号，也不会上传照片。

为什么看不到部分照片？

如果你选择了“有限访问”，GalleryX 只能看到系统允许的照片。你可以前往系统设置调整照片权限。

删除的照片去了哪里？

确认删除后，照片会进入系统相册的“最近删除”，并由系统保留约 30 天。

如何反馈问题？

请附上 GalleryX 版本、iPhone 型号、iOS 版本，以及问题发生前的操作步骤。请勿发送私人照片。

联系 GalleryX 支持

English

GalleryX organizes photos directly on your device. No account is required, and your photos are never uploaded.

Why are some photos missing?

With Limited Photos access, GalleryX can only show photos allowed by the system. You can change photo access in system Settings.

Where do deleted photos go?

After you confirm deletion, photos move to Recently Deleted in Photos and are kept by the system for about 30 days.

How do I report an issue?

Include your GalleryX version, iPhone model, iOS version, and the steps leading to the issue. Please do not send private photos.
